## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## 2. Proposed Solution

### Project Overview

Farm2Market is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, supermarkets, restaurants and bulk buyers directly.

The platform aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain and create a transparent, efficient and affordable marketplace.

### How It Addresses the Problem

- Provides direct communication between farmers and buyers.
- Allows farmers to list their agricultural products.
- Enables consumers to compare product prices.
- Provides bulk purchasing options for businesses.
- Uses AI-based demand forecasting to predict market requirements.
- Supports logistics coordination for efficient delivery.

### Innovation and Uniqueness

- AI-powered agricultural demand prediction.
- Real-time agricultural price comparison.
- Smart buyer-farmer matching.
- Regional language support for rural farmers.
- Intelligent delivery route planning.
- Separate dashboards for farmers, consumers and administrators.

### Methodology and Implementation

1. Farmer registration and authentication.
2. Farmers upload available agricultural products.
3. Product details and prices are stored in the database.
4. Consumers and bulk buyers browse available products.
5. Buyers place orders directly with farmers.
6. AI analyses historical demand and predicts future requirements.
7. Logistics support helps coordinate product delivery.
8. Users can track order status through the platform.

## 4. Feasibility and Viability

### Feasibility Analysis

Farm2Market can be developed as a web-based platform using commonly available technologies.

The initial version can support farmer registration, product listings, buyer registration, order placement and basic price comparison.

Advanced AI and logistics features can be integrated in later development stages.

### Potential Challenges and Risks

| Challenge | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|
| Limited internet access | Lightweight and mobile-friendly website |
| Digital awareness | Simple interface and regional languages |
| Transportation difficulties | Partnerships with local logistics providers |
| Price fluctuations | Regular market price updates |
| Product wastage | AI-based demand forecasting |
| Data reliability | Product verification and regular updates |

### Strategies for Implementation

- Begin with a small group of farmers and buyers.
- Test the platform in a selected local agricultural market.
- Collect user feedback.
- Improve the platform based on actual requirements.
- Expand to additional regions after successful testing.
